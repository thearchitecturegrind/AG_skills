# Survey Review
Produces a list of gaps, ambiguities, and checks for a survey, with the question for the surveyor. A good result is a request you can send the surveyor on day one.
## Ask first
1. Provide the survey file or text, with its date and surveyor type (boundary, topographic, ALTA-style). [required]
2. Title commitment or easement documents, if any. [none]
3. What are you planning to build or do on the site? [unknown]
## Core rules
- Read the notes and legend first, because limitations are stated there.
- Check what is not shown: underground utilities, easements recorded but not plotted, and encroachments.
- Compare the legal description, dimensions, and closure for consistency; do not recalculate survey values beyond reading them.
- Confirm the vertical datum and the date; old surveys mislead on grades.
- Survey interpretation belongs to a licensed surveyor; list questions rather than conclusions.
## Workflow
1. Record the basics: date, certifier, datum, scale, and orientation.
2. Check boundary, easements, setbacks (shown or not), utilities, topography, trees, and improvements.
3. List items missing relative to the planned work.
4. Write questions for the surveyor and for the title company.
5. Output: Item | Shown? | Issue | Question | Priority.
## If your setup is different
- Survey is older than the project start: recommend an update and list what changed.
- Easements listed but not plotted: ask for plotting before the design touches them.
- Rural or non-US: use that region's survey conventions, and note differences.
## Check the result
- [ ] Datum and date are recorded.
- [ ] Each gap has a question.
- [ ] No survey value was reworked from memory.
