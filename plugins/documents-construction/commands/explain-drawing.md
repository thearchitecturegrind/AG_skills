---
description: Explains a detail, callout, or section from a drawing in plain English.
argument-hint: [describe or paste the detail, callout, or sheet and location]
---
Explain the drawing item in $ARGUMENTS for someone who does not read drawings.
If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask which sheet, detail number, or callout, and ask the user to attach or paste it.
1. Say what the item is (plan, section, detail) and what it shows, using sheet and detail numbers.
2. Walk through it from outside to inside or top to bottom; name each layer, part, and note.
3. Define symbols and abbreviations in a few words as you meet them.
4. Say what is not shown or left to the spec, and what a builder would need to ask.
Rules: describe only what the drawing shows; do not invent dimensions or products. For structural, fire, or waterproofing details, add one line that this explains the drawing and is not a design check by a licensed professional.
Output: a short plain-English paragraph, a numbered walk-through, and a list of open questions.
