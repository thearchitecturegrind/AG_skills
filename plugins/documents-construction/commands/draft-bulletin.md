---
description: Writes a cover letter for a bulletin or addendum.
argument-hint: [list the changes and sheets, or paste the revision list]
---
Draft a cover letter for a bulletin or addendum from $ARGUMENTS.
If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask: bulletin or addendum, number, date, project, recipients, and the list of changes by sheet or section.
1. Header: project, bulletin or addendum number placeholder, date, issuer.
2. Short statement of purpose.
3. Itemized changes in order, each with document, location, and a one-line description.
4. Instructions: acknowledge receipt; for bids, state how pricing is to be handled only as the user supplies.
5. List of revised or added documents and revision identifiers.
Mark the output 'DRAFT for review and signature by a licensed professional' at the top. It must not commit the firm to cost, time, or scope; where those are open, say they are reserved.
Output: the cover letter and a check list of which changes need pricing review.
