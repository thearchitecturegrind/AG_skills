# Plan Review
Reads a plan as an argument about how a building is used. A good result lists what the plan clearly decides, what it leaves out, and what its layout implies about use that nobody may have intended.

## Ask first
1. Which level and what use (home, office, clinic)? [if unclear, ask; use drives everything]
2. Do you have scale, north, and room areas, or only a sketch? [if no scale, avoid dimensional claims]
3. Is this for design critique, coordination, or a pre-issue check? [assume design critique]

## Core rules
- Start with how a person moves from entry to each room; a plan that works only on paper fails in use.
- Separate what is drawn (walls, doors) from what is implied (furniture zones, queues, service routes).
- Only state a dimension if it is printed or measurable at a known scale; otherwise say "appears".
- Note missing items that the plan type normally carries: north arrow, scale, door swings, grid, level, room names.
- Accessibility, egress, and fire questions are flagged for the licensed professional, never decided here.

## Workflow
1. Identify entry, main spaces, services, and the circulation spine.
2. Trace three journeys (arrival to main room, to toilet, to exit) and record friction points.
3. Note adjacencies that help or conflict, and rooms lacking outside access or daylight.
4. List omissions and ambiguities by sheet location.
5. List implications: what the layout will make easy and what it will make awkward.
6. Output: Decisions shown, Omissions, Journey findings, Implications, Questions; each item with a location on the plan.

## If your setup is different
- Sketch only: review the idea and the adjacency logic, skip drafting-completeness checks.
- Multi-storey: ask for the section or other levels before judging stairs and cores.
- Interior fit-out plan: weight furniture, services, and finishes over structure.

## Check the result
- [ ] Each finding has a location on the plan.
- [ ] No dimension is stated that is not printed or scaled.
- [ ] Life-safety items are flagged, not judged.
