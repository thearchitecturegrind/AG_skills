# Site Analysis
Produces an organized review of what the site allows, limits, and offers, with the evidence and the missing information. A good result tells the designer what to respect, what to verify, and what to ask.

## Ask first
1. Where is the site and what do you have (survey, photos, zoning documents, title information)? [if none, produce a data-gathering list]
2. What is the intended use and scale? [ask; it decides which factors matter]
3. Are there known constraints (heritage, flood, trees, easements, neighbours)? [assume none known, to be checked]

## Core rules
- Facts about zoning, flood, seismic, wind, climate, soil, and utilities come from official sources you look up with date and link, or from documents the user supplies; otherwise "unverified".
- Separate what you observed or were told from what was researched and from what is assumed.
- Walk the site in the mind: approach, edges, views, noise, sun, wind, drainage; good analysis is visual and spatial, not only a checklist.
- Hazards and geotechnical matters are for specialists; list the investigation needed.
- Every constraint gets a source and a consequence for the design.

## Workflow
1. List the site facts available and their sources.
2. Review factors: legal and planning, access and transport, orientation and climate, topography and drainage, ecology, context and views, services, hazards.
3. Record each as Constraint, Opportunity, or Unknown with a source.
4. Identify the buildable area and the strongest design cues.
5. List verification tasks and consultants required.
6. Output: summary, factor table (finding, source, status, design consequence), buildable area description, and questions and investigations.

## If your setup is different
- Overseas or unfamiliar jurisdiction: ask which authority publishes the rules and work from their documents.
- Urban infill: weigh neighbours, party walls, and overlooking more heavily.
- Rural site: weigh services, access, and natural hazard more heavily.

## Check the result
- [ ] Every finding has a source or is marked unverified.
- [ ] Unknowns are turned into tasks.
- [ ] No design proposal was made.
