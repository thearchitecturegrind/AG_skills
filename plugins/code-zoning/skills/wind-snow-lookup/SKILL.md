# Wind Snow Lookup
Produces a record of site wind and snow design criteria with source and date, plus open inputs. A good result can be defended in review and repeated later.
## Ask first
1. Site address or coordinates, and the risk category. [required]
2. Adopted code and standard editions, or I will find them. [find]
3. Any local amendment or special study, such as local snow load policies. [ask]
## Core rules
- Take values from the official hazard tool or the local authority's published value, never memory; maps and local policies change.
- Record the tool's inputs and the date, because values change with risk category and elevation.
- Check whether the jurisdiction publishes its own snow load or wind speed value that overrides the map.
- Exposure category and drift conditions are engineer's judgment; list them as open inputs.
- Structural design belongs to the engineer of record.
## Workflow
1. Confirm the codes, standards, and editions.
2. Retrieve wind and snow inputs from the official sources, capturing output and date.
3. Check the local authority for override values.
4. List open inputs: exposure, topography, roof configuration.
5. Output: Criterion | Value | Source | Date | Inputs | Open question.
## If your setup is different
- Site is in a special wind region or high-elevation area: flag it for local study.
- Tool is down: provide manual steps and mark unverified.
- Outside the US: use national data and standards.
## Check the result
- [ ] Each value has a source and date.
- [ ] Local overrides were checked.
- [ ] Open inputs are assigned.
