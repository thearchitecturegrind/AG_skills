# Fit-Out Study
Tests whether a required programme can occupy an existing shell and shows the compromises needed. A good result gives a pass, fail, marginal, or cannot-tell verdict with numbers and names the landlord or base-build limits.

## Ask first
1. What are the shell dimensions, column grid, core positions, window lines, and floor load limits? [supply drawings or the base-build document; missing items become questions]
2. What is the programme (rooms, headcount, equipment)? [ask for the schedule]
3. What are the landlord or base-build rules (ceiling height, services capacity, hours, alterations allowed)? [if not supplied, mark "unknown, ask the landlord"]

## Core rules
- Measure usable area from the real boundaries (inside face, core walls, columns) and show the figure; the lease area is rarely the usable area.
- Test services capacity (power, cooling, ventilation, drainage) against the programme using supplied data; do not assume spare capacity.
- Respect the column grid and window module when placing rooms; fighting them costs money.
- Check egress routes and occupant loads against the code you supply; the licensed professional decides compliance.
- Record reinstatement duties at lease end, since they affect cost.

## Workflow
1. Compute net usable area, showing each deduction.
2. Compare the programme area to usable area and give the ratio.
3. Place the largest and most constrained rooms first (labs, server rooms, meeting rooms), then the rest.
4. Test daylight, services reach, and circulation widths with numbers.
5. List compromises and which party must approve them.
6. Output: verdict, area table, test-fit description, services checks, approvals needed, and open questions.

## If your setup is different
- No dimensions: provide the information request list, not a layout.
- Owner-occupied shell: drop landlord items and keep services and structure checks.
- Open-plan versus cellular: run both and compare capacity.

## Check the result
- [ ] The usable area calculation is shown.
- [ ] A verdict is stated with numbers.
- [ ] Landlord approvals are listed.
