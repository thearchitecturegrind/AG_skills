Explain the specification section in $ARGUMENTS in plain language.
If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask for the section text or number and the spec file. Do not explain a section from memory; use the text supplied.
1. State what the section covers and where it sits in the spec (Part 1 General, Part 2 Products, Part 3 Execution).
2. Summarize what the contractor must do, submit, and prove, with paragraph numbers.
3. Translate standards and jargon, in a few words each. Verify any referenced standard's name from the text only.
4. Flag choices (options in brackets), blanks, and anything that conflicts with the drawings if supplied.
5. Say who is responsible for what.
Output: a plain summary, a short obligations list with paragraph numbers, and questions to resolve.
