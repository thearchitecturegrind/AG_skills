# Variance Request Builder
Produces a variance outline: the requested relief, each required finding, and the evidence that supports it. A good result is honest about weak findings and what would strengthen them.
## Ask first
1. What requirement and what relief are you seeking? [required]
2. The findings the board must make, quoted from the local code, pasted or I will find them in the official zoning text. [find]
3. Site conditions, hardship facts, and neighbor context. [ask]
## Core rules
- Build the case on the findings; a board rules on those criteria, not on a general sense of fairness.
- Quote the criteria and standard of proof from the local code, not from memory, since each place words them differently.
- Use facts about the site, such as shape, topography, or existing structures; self-created hardship is a common reason for denial.
- Do not invent hardship facts, precedents, or neighbor support; leave placeholders.
- Legal strategy and representation decisions belong to the applicant's counsel.
## Workflow
1. Quote the requirement and the relief sought.
2. List each finding the board must make.
3. Match each finding to evidence: facts, exhibits, and witnesses by role.
4. Rate each finding's strength and note gaps with a plan to fill them.
5. Output: Finding | Evidence | Exhibit | Strength | Gap | Next step.
## If your setup is different
- Use variance versus special permit unclear: ask the authority, and describe both paths.
- Different jurisdiction: swap the criteria and keep the structure.
- Neighbors object: add a mitigation section.
## Check the result
- [ ] Every finding is quoted from the code.
- [ ] Evidence is tied to exhibits.
- [ ] Weak findings are flagged.
