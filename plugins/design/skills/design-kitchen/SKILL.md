# Design Kitchen
Produces a kitchen layout organized by how cooking actually happens and where services can reach. A good result gives positions and clear distances, with the arithmetic and sources for each clearance.

## Ask first
1. What are the room dimensions, window and door positions, and the locations of drain, water, gas or power, and extract route? [if unknown, mark each service "assumed"]
2. Who cooks, how often, and what appliances are needed? [assume a family of four with a dishwasher and a full-size oven]
3. Which clearance and ventilation rules apply? [supply the local code or appliance data sheets; I will not quote figures from memory]

## Core rules
- Arrange around the work sequence: store, prepare, cook, clean; this reduces steps and crossing of paths.
- Place the sink near the drain and a window if possible; long drain runs are costly and prone to blockage.
- Keep the hob away from door swings and not under a window that opens into a flame zone; place extraction on an exterior wall or short duct run.
- Take appliance sizes and door-open clearances from data sheets, not generic assumptions.
- Gas, electrical, and ventilation work is specified and approved by qualified trades and authorities; flag only.

## Workflow
1. Record the shell and services with dimensions and mark assumptions.
2. List appliances with sizes from data sheets.
3. Compare layout types (galley, L, U, island) in the available space; test the aisle width and door swings with numbers.
4. Place the sink, hob, and fridge; check the work-triangle distances or alternative work zones, showing sums.
5. Allocate storage, worktop lengths, and landing areas beside hob and sink.
6. Output: preferred layout with dimensions, one alternative, clearance checks (pass, fail, marginal), services list, and open questions.

## If your setup is different
- Open-plan kitchen: add sight lines, noise, and smell control.
- Commercial kitchen: ask for the health-authority rules and the equipment schedule first.
- Tight retrofit: fix the services as given and fit units to them.

## Check the result
- [ ] Every clearance has a number and a source.
- [ ] Extract route and drain run are addressed.
- [ ] Door and appliance swings are checked.
