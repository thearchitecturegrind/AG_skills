# Design Narrative
Produces a clear project description that an outsider can follow without drawings. A good result states what was built, why it is shaped that way, and what a visitor would experience, using only facts the team supplied.

## Ask first
1. Who is the reader and where will it appear (jury, website, planning application, press)? [assume an educated non-specialist]
2. What length and tone? [300 words, plain and confident]
3. What facts can I use: location type, size, programme, materials, constraints, outcomes? [if any are missing, leave bracketed placeholders; never invent them]

## Core rules
- Lead with the problem the design answers; readers remember reasons better than features.
- Every number or material named must come from the supplied material; invented specifics destroy trust.
- Describe the experience in the order someone encounters it (approach, entry, main space) so the text follows the building.
- Define any technical term in a few words the first time; the reader was not in the room.
- Claims of performance or awards need a source from the team, or they stay out.

## Workflow
1. Extract the facts and mark each with where it came from.
2. Identify the one central idea and two supporting moves.
3. Draft: context and challenge, response, experience, result.
4. Cut adjectives that do not carry a fact; replace with the fact.
5. Replace any missing fact with a [placeholder: ask the team].
6. Output: the narrative at the requested length, then a list of placeholders and a list of claims needing team confirmation.

## If your setup is different
- Planning or regulatory audience: lead with fit to context and policy; ask the user for the policy wording rather than recalling it.
- Award entry with a word limit: provide a version at the limit and a 50-word summary.
- No facts supplied at all: return a fill-in outline with questions, not prose.

## Check the result
- [ ] A stranger could say what the project is after one read.
- [ ] No fact appears that the user did not supply.
- [ ] Placeholders are visible and listed.
