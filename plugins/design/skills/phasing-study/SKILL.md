# Phasing Study
Proposes a sequence of work areas and the temporary arrangements needed to keep the building working. A good result shows each phase, who is displaced, which services are interrupted, and what must be true before moving on.

## Ask first
1. What must stay operational, for whom, and with what tolerance for noise, dust, and outages? [ask; this is the main constraint]
2. What is the scope by area and the overall time window? [if none, produce a structure with durations left blank]
3. Do you have swing space, and can tenants or services move temporarily? [assume none]

## Core rules
- Start from the people and functions that cannot stop, then place work around them; a technically logical sequence that closes a critical function is wrong.
- Identify each service shared across phases (power, water, fire alarm, ventilation) and plan temporary connections.
- Keep egress and fire protection of occupied areas continuous; the licensed professional and authority approve temporary arrangements.
- Separate dusty and noisy work from occupied zones with physical barriers and timing.
- Durations come from the contractor or the user, not from memory; blank them if unknown.

## Workflow
1. List the building zones, occupants, and critical functions.
2. Define phases so each hands over a usable area before the next starts.
3. For each phase list work, displaced users, temporary routes, service shutdowns, and protection.
4. Mark dependencies and the decant moves between phases.
5. Identify risks: hidden conditions, late deliveries, shutdown windows.
6. Output: phase table (scope, occupied areas, temporary works, service impacts, durations or blanks), a sequence diagram description, and decisions needed from the owner.

## If your setup is different
- Hospital or school: add stricter infection control or term-time windows from the owner's rules.
- Fully vacated building: replace phasing with a simple sequence and drop temporary arrangements.
- Tenant remains in one wing: define the boundary wall and the access path first.

## Check the result
- [ ] Every phase lists affected occupants and services.
- [ ] Egress continuity is flagged.
- [ ] Durations are sourced or blank.
