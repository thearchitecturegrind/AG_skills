# Design Bathroom
Produces a fixture layout that uses the plumbing wall efficiently and leaves usable clearances. A good result gives dimensions, shows the arithmetic, and states which clearances still need checking against local rules.

## Ask first
1. What are the room's internal dimensions, door position, and where are the existing or planned drainage and water lines? [if unknown, assume one wet wall and label it]
2. Which fixtures do you want, and who uses the room (family, guests, an accessible bathroom)? [assume toilet, basin, shower or bath]
3. What clearances apply? [supply your local code or fixture maker's sheets; I will not quote clearances from memory]

## Core rules
- Group fixtures on one wall or back-to-back walls; shorter pipe runs cost less and reduce noise and risk of leaks.
- Place the toilet first, since its drain is the least flexible; falls to the stack limit how far it can move.
- Draw the door swing and check it does not strike a fixture or the person using it; consider sliding or outward-opening doors.
- Allow floor space for use, not just fixture footprints; take dimensions from the fixture data sheet.
- Accessible bathrooms must be checked against the governing standard by a qualified person.

## Workflow
1. Record the room dimensions, openings, and wet-wall location.
2. Look up or receive fixture sizes and required clearances; cite the source.
3. Place toilet, then basin, then shower or bath; test clearances with simple sums, shown step by step.
4. Test door swing and a person's path from door to each fixture.
5. Note ventilation, waterproofing, and access for pipes.
6. Output: dimensioned sketch description or table, clearance checks (pass, fail, marginal, with numbers), alternatives if it fails.

## If your setup is different
- Small room: compare a shower-only version to a bath version and say what is lost.
- Different regulations or units: use the user's table and keep one unit.
- Retrofit with fixed drains: place fixtures to the existing positions first, then test.

- [ ] Each clearance cites its source.
- [ ] Door swing is checked.
- [ ] Pipe runs are short and noted.
