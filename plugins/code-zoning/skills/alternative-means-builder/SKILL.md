# Alternative Means Builder
Produces a written case: the rule, its intent, the alternative, the evidence of equivalence, and the compensating measures. A good result lets a reviewer say yes without doing the research themselves.
## Ask first
1. Which rule is the alternative departing from? Paste it, or I will fetch it from an official source. [required]
2. Describe the alternative and why the prescriptive route is hard here. [required]
3. Does your authority have a stated process or form for alternatives? [I will look for it]
## Core rules
- State the rule's intent from the code's own purpose or commentary where available; guessing intent weakens the case.
- Show equivalence with evidence the reviewer can check, such as tests, listings, calculations, or precedent; bare assertion rarely persuades.
- Name compensating measures, since a reviewer wants to see what makes up for the departure.
- Do not invent test results, precedents, or approvals; leave placeholders for evidence that has not been gathered.
- Life safety content is for a licensed professional and the authority to decide; this is a drafting aid.
## Workflow
1. Quote the prescriptive requirement and its source.
2. Write the intent in two sentences, citing where the intent comes from.
3. Describe the alternative, then lay out an equivalence argument with each piece of evidence and its status.
4. List compensating measures and any conditions you would accept.
5. Output: Rule | Intent | Proposal | Evidence table (item, source, status) | Compensating measures | Questions for the authority.
## If your setup is different
- Authority requires a form: map my sections to its fields rather than replacing it.
- No evidence yet: produce an evidence-gathering plan, not a finished case.
- Performance-based path is allowed: note that the argument becomes an analysis, and suggest involving the relevant specialist.
## Check the result
- [ ] Intent and evidence each carry a source or a placeholder.
- [ ] Compensating measures are specific.
- [ ] No claims of prior approval unless the user provided them.
