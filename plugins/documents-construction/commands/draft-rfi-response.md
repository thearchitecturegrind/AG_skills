description: Answers an RFI without committing to scope, cost, or time.
argument-hint: [paste the RFI and the relevant sheets or sections]
---
Draft a response to the RFI in $ARGUMENTS.
If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask for the RFI text, relevant drawings and spec sections, and the intended answer.
1. Quote the question briefly.
2. Answer directly, citing sheets and sections that govern.
3. If the answer needs a drawing change, say a revision or bulletin will follow rather than describing it as final.
4. Add the reservation that the response does not change the contract sum or time; if the contractor believes it does, they follow the contract's notice process. Ask for the clause; do not cite it from memory.
5. List attachments (sketches).
Mark the output 'DRAFT for review and signature by a licensed professional' at the top. It must not commit the firm to cost, time, or scope; where those are open, say they are reserved.
Output: the response text, then a note to the reviewer on whether the question reveals a document error.
