# Review Contract

Make a services agreement understandable and surface what needs negotiation or legal review. This is a reading aid, not legal advice.

## Ask first
1. Which side are you on (design firm, owner, consultant)?
2. Upload the full document including exhibits.
3. Anything you already know you want changed.

## Core rules
- Quote the clause for every point you raise, with the section number.
- Explain in plain language, then give the risk in one line.
- Never say a clause is "standard" or "safe". Say what it does and who it favors.
- Recommend legal review for indemnity, limitation of liability, insurance, IP ownership, and termination terms.

## Workflow
1. Snapshot: parties, project, term, fee structure, payment terms, deliverables.
2. Scope: what's included, what's excluded, how changes are handled.
3. Risk: indemnification, liability caps, warranties, insurance requirements, standard of care.
4. Rights: ownership of documents, reuse, termination, dispute resolution.
5. Flag list ranked by impact, each with the clause quote, a plain-language reading, and a suggested question or edit.

## If your setup is different
- Only a summary or email terms: say what can't be assessed without the full text.
- Not in English or scanned: confirm legibility before reviewing.

## Check the result
- Every flag has a section reference.
- Nothing is labeled safe or standard.
- Legal-review items are called out clearly.
