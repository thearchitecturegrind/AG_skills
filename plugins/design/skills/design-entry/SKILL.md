# Design Entry
Treats the entry as a series of steps, not a door, and lays out each one. A good result lets someone walk the sequence mentally and see where they stop, wait, get wet, or get lost.

## Ask first
1. What type of building and who arrives (public, residents, staff, deliveries), and how (foot, car, wheelchair, bicycle)? [ask; it changes every step]
2. What is the climate and site condition at the entry (rain, snow, wind, level change)? [if unknown, assume a cold wet climate and label it]
3. Is there a security or control need (reception, access control)? [assume light control]

## Core rules
- Design the sequence: street, approach, threshold, door one, vestibule or lobby, door two; each step has one job.
- Provide shelter and landing space outside the door, since people stop there to find keys or close umbrellas.
- Make the way in obvious from the street through form, light, or signage, so no one searches.
- Use a vestibule between two doors where heat, wind, or security demand it, sized for door swings and a wheelchair or pram.
- Accessibility dimensions, slopes, and door hardware come from the governing standard that you supply or I look up; the licensed professional confirms.

## Workflow
1. Describe the arrival journey for each user group.
2. Define each stage: position, approximate size, function, and risk (weather, queueing, conflict).
3. Size the vestibule from door swing plus clearance; use supplied dimensions and show the sum.
4. Place mats, benches, intercoms, mailboxes, and bikes so they do not block the path.
5. Check how a delivery or a stretcher passes.
6. Output: sequence table (stage, purpose, size, issue), plan notes, and a list of items to confirm.

## If your setup is different
- Existing building: start from the door you cannot move and work outward.
- Mixed-use entry: separate lobbies by user, then decide what they share.
- Tropical setting: replace the vestibule logic with shade, rain, and ventilation.

## Check the result
- [ ] Every stage has a purpose and a size or a placeholder.
- [ ] Both door swings are tested.
- [ ] Accessibility is flagged for review.
