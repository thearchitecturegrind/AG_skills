# Section Review
Reads a section as the place where the building's vertical logic must work. A good result says which vertical problems the section solves, which it avoids, and where it contradicts the plan or elevation.

## Ask first
1. Which section line, and do you have the plan and elevation it cuts? [if not, say consistency cannot be checked]
2. Are dimensions shown, and what is the intended structure and services approach? [if unstated, mark as assumed]
3. Is it concept or documentation level? [concept: test the idea; documentation: test the build-up]

## Core rules
- Add up the vertical stack (finish, structure, ceiling void, services) and compare with floor-to-floor; this is where sections most often fail.
- Check where the section is cut: a cut that avoids the hard spot (stair, plant, level change) proves little.
- Look at how the section meets the ground, the roof, and the neighbours; edges are where problems hide.
- Daylight and ventilation claims need depth and opening heights from the drawing, not impressions.
- Structural depth, fire separation, and headroom requirements are for the licensed professional; flag, do not decide.

## Workflow
1. List what the section decides: levels, heights, spans, roof form, openings.
2. Do the stack arithmetic for each floor, showing numbers read from the drawing.
3. Compare cut position, levels, and openings with plan and elevation.
4. List what is not shown (services routes, drainage falls, insulation line, waterproofing).
5. Suggest where a second section would reveal more.
6. Output: Resolved, Open, Stack arithmetic, Cross-check mismatches (with sheet refs), Extra sections needed.

## If your setup is different
- No dimensions: discuss proportion and relationships only, and ask for heights.
- Site section: focus on levels, slopes, and neighbours rather than build-up.
- Sketch section: review the spatial idea and list what must be developed.

## Check the result
- [ ] Stack arithmetic is shown, with its source on the drawing.
- [ ] Mismatches cite both sheets.
- [ ] Structural and life-safety points are flagged only.
