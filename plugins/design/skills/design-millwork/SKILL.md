# Design Millwork
Produces a clear description of a built-in so a joiner can price it and the builder can prepare the walls. A good result lists every part, material, finish, hardware item, and the support that must exist before installation.

## Ask first
1. What is the piece, where does it sit, and what must it hold (books, clothes, equipment)? [ask for dimensions and the loads]
2. What quality and finish level, and any chosen materials or sheet sizes? [if none, propose options and label them]
3. Who prices and builds it, and what drawing format do they expect? [assume a joiner needing elevations, plans, and a section]

## Core rules
- Give overall sizes and tolerances against measured walls; rooms are rarely square, so state the scribing allowance.
- Specify the support: blocking, fixing method, wall type behind; heavy cabinets fixed to plasterboard alone often fail.
- List hardware by function (hinge type, runners, soft close) with supplier data sheets, not guessed models.
- Call out finish, edge treatment, grain direction, and joint locations because they drive price and look.
- Load ratings, fixings capacity, and fire-rated or accessibility conditions are for the responsible professional; flag, do not decide.

## Workflow
1. Record dimensions, site conditions, and loads.
2. Break the piece into carcases, shelves, doors, drawers, and trim; give each a material and thickness.
3. Add the cutting list totals only if sizes are supplied; show the arithmetic.
4. Define fixings and wall preparation, such as blocking positions with dimensions.
5. List hardware, finishes, and services (lighting, power, ventilation).
6. Output: part schedule, hardware list, support requirements for the builder, items to survey on site, questions for the joiner.

## If your setup is different
- Wet area: require moisture-rated materials and sealing details.
- Existing wall of unknown construction: add an opening-up check before final sizes.
- Metric versus imperial supply: keep one unit and note standard sheet sizes from the supplier.

## Check the result
- [ ] Wall support is stated, not assumed.
- [ ] Hardware references come from supplied or sourced data.
- [ ] Site measurement is listed as a step.
